The Three Tiers

What The Labels Mean

Actionable

Changes your care today

A diagnosis, a medication decision, a referral, or a treatment already in motion. Examples our physician commonly orders in this tier include home sleep apnea testing for suspected OSA, office spirometry for respiratory symptoms, and metabolic labs that inform an active treatment plan.

Trackable

Builds your baseline

Metrics whose value comes from the trend, not the single number — cardiopulmonary fitness (VO₂ max), body composition, and metabolic markers followed at a sensible cadence.

Exploratory

Lower-yield or emerging

Markers with thinner evidence or narrower applicability. We tell you when something falls here.

These examples illustrate our framework — they are not a complete test menu, and every recommendation is individualized during your consultation.
