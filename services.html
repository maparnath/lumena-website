Reading Your Plan

What Belongs On Your Plan, And Why

Some results directly shift a decision — a diagnosis, a medication, a referral, or a treatment already underway. Others are worth following over months or years, since the value comes from the trend rather than a single number. And when a marker is newer or lower-yield, we're transparent about that too — available if you want it, never bundled in silently.

Good Faith Estimate. LUMENA is a cash- and credit-only practice — we do not bill insurance. Under the No Surprises Act, you'll receive a written Good Faith Estimate of expected costs before your visit. Ask us for one any time, even before scheduling.
