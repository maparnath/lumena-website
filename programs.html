What We Treat

Four Ways We Help You Get Healthier.

Independent pulmonary opinions, physician-supervised metabolic and weight care, and objective functional testing — every recommendation labeled by the evidence behind it. Cash and credit only.

Weight, Obesity & Metabolic Health

Getting healthier isn't only about the number on the scale. We build your plan around metabolic health at your current weight, and support medically appropriate weight loss when that's the right next step — same physician-led process either way.

Physician-Supervised Program

Medical Weight-Loss Program

For patients who qualify after a physician evaluation, FDA-approved weight-loss medication can be part of a monitored program: regular follow-up, lab tracking, body-composition checks and dose adjustments, with physician oversight throughout.

A medical evaluation is required before any treatment decision. Individual results vary.

Functional Assessment

Objective testing — cardiopulmonary exercise testing (VO₂ max), body composition, pulmonary function, and more. Each test is labeled and priced before you agree to it.

Pulmonary Consultation

Breathlessness, a cough that will not go away, or a second opinion on an existing lung or sleep diagnosis. No referral needed. Read personally by your physician, not summarized by staff. Available to patients not currently under this physician's insurance-based care, to keep every review free of conflict of interest.
