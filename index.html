What we do

Modern Medicine For Weight, Metabolism And Fitness

We measure what is happening inside your body, explain it plainly, and treat it with approaches backed by published evidence.

Weight And Obesity Medicine

Medical weight loss led by a physician. When appropriate, FDA-approved medication is part of a plan that also protects muscle, strength and metabolic health.

Metabolic Health

Blood sugar, cholesterol, blood pressure, fatty liver and sleep apnea evaluated together, because they rarely travel alone.

Functional Assessment

VO2 max and exercise testing, body composition and lung function: objective measures of how well your body works, repeated to track progress.

Also available: pulmonary consultation for breathlessness and chronic cough, no referral needed.

How it works

Three Ways To Care

Start with a visit or with your results. Then, if you want, stay with us for ongoing care.

Path 1

Start with a visit

Best if something is wrong and you want it looked at.

  1. Step 01Come in and tell usWe go through your symptoms, history and concerns.
  2. Step 02You are examinedA physician examines you and takes your measurements.
  3. Step 03We order your testsOnly what your evaluation calls for.
  4. Step 04We go through the resultsYou come back, we explain what we found, and treatment starts.

Path 2

Start with your results

Best if you would rather have the numbers in hand before you sit down.

  1. Step 01Order your testing up frontYou choose your testing, pay for that testing, and it is ordered straight away.
  2. Step 02Get testedAt our office or a partner location.
  3. Step 03Come in with your resultsA physician has read them before you arrive, then goes through them with you.
  4. Step 04Measurements and anything furtherBiometrics are done at this visit, along with any additional testing you want. The remaining fees are paid here.

Path 3

Stay with us

Best if you want continuing support after your evaluation.

  1. Step 01Begin with Path 1 or Path 2Every plan starts with a physician evaluation.
  2. Step 02Choose ongoing careRegular follow-up on a monthly basis, arranged around your goals.
  3. Step 03Measure, adjust, repeatProgress is tracked and your plan is adjusted as you go.

What People Ask First

What does LUMENA treat?

Obesity and medical weight loss, metabolic health, and functional assessment such as VO2 max, body composition and exercise testing. Pulmonary consultation for breathlessness and chronic cough is also available.

Do you prescribe weight-loss medication?

When appropriate after a physician evaluation, FDA-approved medications may be part of your plan. Medication is one tool inside a broader plan that protects muscle, metabolic health and fitness.

Do you take insurance?

No. We are cash and credit only, with transparent pricing. You know what something costs before you agree to it.

Do I need a referral?

No referral is needed.

Do you offer ongoing care?

Yes. After your evaluation, you can continue with regular physician-led follow-up. Ask us about the options.
