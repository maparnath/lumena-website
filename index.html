Our Philosophy

We Don't Sell Panels. We Explain Them.

Every test on a LUMENA plan earns its place. Before anything reaches your care plan, a board-certified physician weighs it against one question: does this actually change what we do for you? We could order a hundred tests — we stratify, so what lands on your plan is what's worth ordering, and you know exactly why it's there.

This is the same discipline applied to a severe airflow obstruction on a spirometry loop, an elevated Apo B hiding behind a normal-looking cholesterol panel, or a finding on a bronchoscopy image — read by the physician who's seeing you, not a lab algorithm.
