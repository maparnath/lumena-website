About LUMENA

Physician-Led. Independently Run.

LUMENA exists because metabolic and pulmonary health deserve the same evidence discipline as hospital medicine — without an insurance company deciding what counts.

Meet Your Physician

Directly Involved In Your Care.

LUMENA was founded and is directly run by a practicing pulmonary and critical care physician, whose day job is managing the sickest version of these diseases in the hospital. That is the standard applied here. Every care plan is set and read by your physician — not handed to a health coach or an algorithm.

Why We Exist

Cash or Card. Clear Prices. Physician-Led Care.

Simple, Direct Payment

LUMENA is a cash and credit card practice; we do not bill insurance. You know the price of a visit or test before you agree to it, and your care plan is set by your physician.

A Self-Imposed Standard

Each recommendation we make is labeled Actionable, Trackable or Exploratory.

Genuinely Physician-Led

Your consultation, your plan, and your follow-up are handled by your physician — the same person, start to finish.
